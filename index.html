How to play

Look at the dot in the middle. A vehicle appears in the centre and a yellow diamond sign appears somewhere on the ring, then a pattern hides them.

First choose which vehicle you saw, then tap where the diamond was. Get both right and the flash gets shorter; miss and it gets longer.

Keys: Space/Enter to start, ←/→ or A/D for the vehicle, 1–8 (clockwise from the top), arrow keys or the numpad for the position, M to mute.

Inspired by useful-field-of-view training used in research; not medical advice.
